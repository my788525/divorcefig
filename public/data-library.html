2026 Child Support Models & Percentage-of-Income Tables by State

All 50 states + DC classified by child support calculation model (Income Shares, Percentage of Income, or Melson Formula). The six Percentage-of-Income states have explicit statutory percentage tables; the three Melson Formula states (DE, HI, MT) apply a self-support reserve layer. Income Shares states each publish a state-specific 'Schedule of Basic Obligations' that must be sourced from that state's IV-D agency — only the model classification is provided here, not the dollar schedules.
